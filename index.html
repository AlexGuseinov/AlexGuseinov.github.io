About

I work on symmetric-key cryptography for constrained and IoT devices. My PhD at AzTU is supervised by Prof. Yadigar Imamverdiyev. I design and analyse lightweight block ciphers (including my own cipher, Dipper), and study vectorial Boolean functions over F2n: APN functions, differential uniformity, invariant attacks and multiplicative complexity.

Much of my work is computer-assisted: exhaustive searches, SAT certificates, hardware synthesis benchmarks and reproducible open-source artefacts.

Research interests

  • Lightweight block cipher design and cryptanalysis
  • Vectorial Boolean functions, S-boxes, APN functions
  • Side-channel and fault behaviour of cipher implementations
  • Hardware and microcontroller cost of cryptographic primitives
