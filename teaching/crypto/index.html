Interactive algorithm explainers

Step-by-step visual walkthroughs of the algorithms covered in lectures. They run in the browser, no installation needed.

Mathematical background

Algebra · before the ciphers
Numbers, groups, rings and fields
From the counting numbers to finite (Galois) fields: what a group, a ring and a field are, and how GF(p) and GF(2⁸) compute.
Number theory · before RSA
gcd, inverses, φ(n), Fermat and Euler
Euclid’s algorithm, the inverse modulo n, the extended Euclidean algorithm with its Bézout coefficients, Euler’s φ function and the theorems of Fermat and Euler, with a calculator for division with remainder.
Euclid · from zero, with practice
Remainders and Euclid’s algorithm
Division with remainder, common divisors, Euclid’s algorithm line by line, the extended algorithm as a table and by substituting back, the inverse modulo n, exercises that check every line you write, and a calculator that gives the remainder, the gcd and the inverse at once.

Classical ciphers

Classical · shift cipher
Caesar cipher
Shift every letter by k on a cipher wheel, and break it by trying all 26 keys.
Classical · multiply and shift
Affine cipher
c = (a·p + b) mod 26: why a must have no common factor with 26, and how a⁻¹ decrypts.
Classical · polyalphabetic
Vigenère cipher
A key word gives a new shift for every letter: the Vigenère table, and why the repeating key is its weakness.
Classical · one-time pad
Vernam cipher
XOR every bit with a random key: perfect secrecy, and what goes wrong when a key is used twice.
Cryptanalysis · Kasiski 1863
Breaking Vigenère
Repeated pieces give the key length; then every column is a Caesar cipher, broken by letter frequencies.

Stream ciphers

LFSR · building block
LFSR, step by step
Taps, feedback and period; the LFSR as a stream cipher; and how Berlekamp–Massey breaks one LFSR from 2n bits.
A5/1 · GSM
A5/1 interactive demo
How GSM turns a 64-bit key and a frame number into keystream.

Block ciphers

Lecture 04 · DES
DES, the flow
Feistel rounds, the inside of f, S-boxes and the key schedule.
AES · FIPS-197
AES, step by step
SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, AddRoundKey, the key expansion and diffusion.
PRESENT · lightweight
PRESENT, step by step
The 4-bit S-box, the bit permutation, the 80/128-bit key schedule and diffusion.
Dipper · lightweight SPN–ARX
Dipper, step by step
GIFT S-boxes, word rotations, modular additions with carries, the bit permutation and the key schedule.
Modes · ECB to GCM
Modes of operation, step by step
How a block cipher encrypts a whole message: ECB, CBC, CTR, CFB, OFB and GCM, why an IV is needed, bit errors and four real attacks.

Public-key cryptography

RSA · public key
RSA, step by step
Why public keys exist, the maths behind RSA, real 2048-bit keys, factoring, and the attacks that make padding necessary.
Discrete log · Diffie–Hellman
Discrete log, step by step
Diffie–Hellman, groups and generators, the man in the middle, three ways to attack the log, Pohlig–Hellman and ElGamal.
Elliptic curves · ECDH
Elliptic curves, step by step
The curve and how two of its points are added, the same rules over GF(p), multiples of a point and double-and-add, the grid glued into a 3D doughnut, Diffie–Hellman on a curve, and the real curves P-256, secp256k1 and Curve25519.
Signatures · RSA, DSA, ECDSA
Digital signatures, step by step
Why a MAC is not enough, RSA, Elgamal, DSA and ECDSA signing and verification with real numbers, forgeries that a hash stops, and the private key that leaks when k is used twice.

Post-quantum cryptography

Lattices · LWE · ML-KEM
Lattices and post-quantum cryptography
Lattices in 3D (three.js) that you can turn, good and bad bases, the shortest and closest vector problems, learning with errors with small numbers, and ML-KEM, the NIST standard of 2024.

Hash functions

Hash · SHA-1 · SHA-256
Hash functions, step by step
What a hash must give, the three security properties, the birthday attack, padding and the Merkle–Damgård chain, the SHA-1 schedule and rounds, and why MD5 and SHA-1 were broken.

Protocols

Protocols · authentication · zero knowledge
Protocols and authentication, step by step
What a protocol is and who takes part, passwords and their attacks, Lamport’s one-time passwords, the zero-knowledge cave and the Fiat–Shamir protocol with real numbers.
TLS 1.3 · HTTPS · X25519MLKEM768
TLS 1.3, step by step
The handshake message by message with keys made in your browser, X25519 key exchange, the HKDF key schedule checked against the RFC test values, certificates and CertificateVerify, AES-GCM records, and the post-quantum hybrid that browsers use today.

Blockchain

Blockchain · Bitcoin · Ethereum
Blockchain, step by step
Blocks and hash pointers, transactions, Merkle trees, proof of work, forks, the 51% attack and proof of stake, then how Bitcoin and Ethereum work, with worked examples and references.
